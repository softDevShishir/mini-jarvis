# mini-jarvis: AI advisors for your business

Running a small business leaves little time to read reports. mini-jarvis gives you AI "advisors", called agents. Each agent watches one part of your business and tells you what needs attention, as a short list of suggestions instead of a pile of numbers.

## You stay in control

An agent never acts on its own. It only suggests.

1. The agent looks at your figures and writes a recommendation.
2. You read it and choose **Approve** or **Reject**.
3. Every step is written to a log you can review.

**Example:** The agent suggests "Call Coastal Builders about their overdue invoice." You approve it, and the log records that. Approving only records your decision. Nothing is sent and no money moves. You still make the call.

Also built in:

- **Checked figures.** A dollar amount not found in your data is flagged for review.
- **Cost controls.** There is a daily limit on AI use. Scheduled runs are off unless you turn them on.
- **Your choice of AI.** Claude, Gemini or OpenAI, so you are not locked in.
- **Optional alerts.** New recommendations can be sent to you on Telegram. WhatsApp is planned.

## Available today

**Cash-flow agent.** It looks at your unpaid and overdue invoices, and the last 30 days of money in and money out. It suggests which invoices to chase and where cash is getting tight.

Connecting your own software needs your permission and access. It is done step by step, starting **read-only**: mini-jarvis can look but cannot change anything.

The first step is a short paid review of what data you have and where it lives. Cost: [price]. Length: [time].

## Your data and privacy

- The data needed for an answer is sent to the AI provider chosen for you.
- You can choose which provider is used.
- mini-jarvis does not use your data for anything else.
- Each provider has its own terms. Please read them.
- I recommend leaving out customer personal details, such as phone numbers, unless an agent needs them.

## What this is not

- It is **not a replacement** for your accountant, bookkeeper or manager.
- It does **not act on its own**. It never sends messages, pays bills or changes records.
- AI can be wrong. That is why every recommendation waits for your approval.
